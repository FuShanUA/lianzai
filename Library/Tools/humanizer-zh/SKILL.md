# Humanizer-zh Skill

This skill provides the **expert knowledge** and tools to process text and remove "AI flavor" (translationese, repetitive patterns, lack of soul), making it sound more natural and human-written.

## User Guide
The detailed guidelines on what constitutes "AI writing" and how to fix it are in [README.md](./README.md).

## Usage
The instructions in `README.md` should be applied when:
1. Translating or post-editing Machine Translation outputs.
2. Reviewing content generated by other LLMs.
3. Asked to "humanize" text.

## Rules Summary
1. **No Long Dashes (——)**: Strictly forbidden. Replace with commas (，), colons (：), or periods (。) to fit natural Chinese flow.
2. **Block "AI Words"**: Avoid `此外`, `总而言之`, `不可或缺`, `不仅...而且`. Use more conversational transitions.
3. **Vary Sentence Length**: Mix short, punchy sentences with longer, flowing ones. Avoid uniform structure.

## Visual Direction (If Generating Illustrations)
- **Style**: Warm, bright, "Nano Banana Pro" style (beige background, pastel orange/teal).
- **Tone**: Approachable, clear, direct metaphors. Avoid abstract/cold sci-fi unless requested.
- **Goal**: Make complex concepts instantly understandable at a glance.
